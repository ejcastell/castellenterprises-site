Staff Text Alerts

Operations text messages for Castell Enterprises owners and managers

What this is

Castell Enterprises, Inc. sends operations text messages to its own owners and managers: a daily summary, alerts about company reports, and replies to questions they text in. This program is for our staff only. We do not use it to text guests or the public, and we do not send marketing.

How to enroll

Enrollment is limited to Castell Enterprises owners and managers, and only at their own request. A staff member enrolls by giving us their mobile number with the written consent below, or by texting START to the alerts number they are given.

Consent

By enrolling, you agree to receive recurring operations text messages from Castell Enterprises, Inc. at the mobile number you provide. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to cancel at any time, or HELP for help. Mobile carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Stop or get help

  • Reply STOP to any message to stop receiving texts. You will get one message confirming it.
  • Reply HELP for help, or email ejc@castellenterprises.com.

Privacy

Mobile numbers, text messaging opt-in data and consent collected for this program are not sold, rented or shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. See the Text Messaging Privacy Policy and the Terms and Conditions.
