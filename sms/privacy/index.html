Text Messaging Privacy Policy

Castell Enterprises staff text alerts · Last updated: November 14, 2026

This policy covers the Castell Enterprises, Inc. staff text alert program: operations text messages sent to our own owners and managers. Program details are on the Staff Text Alerts page and in the Terms and Conditions.

Information we collect

  • The staff member's name and mobile phone number.
  • A record of their consent to receive text messages, and of any opt-out.
  • The text messages sent and received, with the date and time of each.

How we use it

Only to send and answer operations messages: daily summaries, alerts about company reports, and replies to questions the staff member texts in. We do not use it for marketing.

How we share it

Mobile information and text messaging consent are not shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes, and they are not sold or rented. This information is shared only with the service providers that deliver the messages (our messaging provider and mobile carriers), and when required by law.

Message frequency and rates

Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply.

Your choices

  • Reply STOP to any message to stop receiving texts.
  • Reply HELP for help.
  • Ask us to access, correct or delete your information by emailing ejc@castellenterprises.com.
