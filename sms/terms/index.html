Text Messaging Terms and Conditions

Castell Enterprises staff text alerts · Last updated: November 14, 2026

Program

Castell Enterprises, Inc. sends operations text messages to its own owners and managers: a daily summary, alerts about company reports, and replies to questions they text in. The program is for our staff only. It is not used to text guests or the public, and it does not send marketing.

Consent

Enrollment is limited to Castell Enterprises owners and managers, at their own request. A staff member enrolls by giving us their mobile number with written consent, or by texting START to the alerts number they are given. By enrolling, you agree to receive recurring operations text messages from Castell Enterprises, Inc. at the mobile number you provide. Consent applies to this program only.

Message frequency

Message frequency varies.

Cost

Message and data rates may apply, depending on your mobile plan.

Stop

Reply STOP to any message to cancel. You will receive one message confirming that you are unsubscribed. Reply START to enroll again.

Help

Reply HELP for help, or email ejc@castellenterprises.com.

Carriers

Mobile carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Privacy

Mobile information and text messaging consent are not shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. See the Text Messaging Privacy Policy.
