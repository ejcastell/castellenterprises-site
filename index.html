Castell Catering is our central commissary on Le Jeune Road, where our culinary team makes our signature items from scratch. Our croqueta masa and empanada fillings are made from scratch with our own recipes. We also make Bored Cuban's pop tarts and every dessert and sauce we serve, down to our house-made mayo.

It's also where we hand-cut and marinate our churrasco, palomilla, chicken and pork, and slice the ham, turkey, chorizo and cheese for every sandwich. Making it ourselves is how we keep quality, food safety and consistency the same at every location.
